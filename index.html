Sentinel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Sentinel is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, security, and server management features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sentinel may access and how that information may be used.

2. Information We May Access

Depending on the features being used, Sentinel may access information required for its operation, including:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Channel and role IDs.
  • Information required to execute commands and moderation features.

3. Use of Information

Information accessible to Sentinel is used only to provide and operate the bot's features, including moderation, security, and server management.

4. Data Retention

Sentinel does not retain personal information for longer than necessary to provide its features.

5. Data Sharing

Information accessible through Sentinel is not sold to third parties and is not used for advertising purposes.

6. Data Deletion

If you wish to request the deletion of information that may be stored by Sentinel, you can contact the Sentinel team through its official Discord server.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Sentinel or its features.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Sentinel team through its official Discord server.
